Silly Bus AI

Everything you have to do, in one place.

Canvas assignments and the homework your teachers type into their course docs, ranked by what's due soonest. Runs entirely on your own Mac — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Install it

Open Terminal (press ⌘Space, type terminal, press Return), paste this line, and press Return.

curl -fsSL https://jomonkey22.github.io/schooldash-install/install.sh | bash

That's the whole install. It downloads Silly Bus AI, puts it in your Applications folder, adds it to your Dock, and opens it — about a minute, and it never asks for your password.

Why a command and not a download button? macOS blocks apps downloaded through a browser unless the developer pays Apple $99 a year — you'd get “Apple could not verify Silly Bus AI is free of malware” and have to dig through System Settings to override it. Files fetched this way aren't flagged, so Silly Bus AI just opens. Same app, no warning.

Want to read the script before running it? It's plain text at jomonkey22.github.io/schooldash-install/install.sh — opening that link shows it rather than downloading it.

What happens next

  1. Silly Bus AI downloads what it needs — a few minutes, with a notification when it is done — then opens its window on the Setup screen.
  2. Save your school Google sign-in there and press Connect. Silly Bus AI signs in to Canvas and Veracross for your own account — now, and whenever a session runs out later. If Google asks for a phone tap, a window opens for that one step.
  3. Check your classes: textbook, syllabus, the teacher's homework doc. Then the first pull, and you're done.
  4. After that, open it from the Dock whenever you like. It refreshes itself every half hour; Refresh pulls the latest.

Add the Chrome extension optional

Silly Bus AI signs in to Canvas and Veracross by itself, in a browser of its own. That works — but a school Google account is awkward to sign in to automatically, and a Veracross session expires about every half hour.

Install this and the Chrome you are already signed in to hands those pages over instead. No sign-in, no session to keep alive, nothing typed.

Install Silly Bus AI first and you do not need to download anything here. The extension ships inside the app. Open Silly Bus AI → Settings → Connect Chrome and it walks you through it in three steps, with a button that opens the right folder in Finder for you.
Rather do it by hand? (or haven't installed the app yet)
Download the extension a small .zip — under 20 KB
  1. Unzip it. You get a folder called sillybus-chrome — keep it somewhere you won't delete, like Documents. Chrome remembers where you loaded it from, so moving or binning that folder stops the extension working.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions — paste that into the address bar, a link can't open it — and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick that folder.
  4. In Silly Bus AI, open Settings → Connect Chrome and press Create a pairing code. Copy it.
  5. Click the Silly Bus AI icon in Chrome's toolbar, paste the code, and press Pair this browser.
Why isn't it in the Chrome Web Store? Publishing there needs a developer account and a review that takes days. Loading it yourself works exactly the same. Chrome shows a small "Developer mode extensions" note when it starts — that's expected, and you can dismiss it.

What it can see. Only Canvas, Veracross and 127.0.0.1 — the five addresses are listed in its manifest.json, which you can read before installing. It fetches the pages Silly Bus AI asks for, using the sign-in already in your browser, and posts them to Silly Bus AI on your own Mac. It never sees a password, there is no account, and nothing goes to anyone else. Silly Bus AI keeps working exactly as before without it.
Would rather not use Terminal?

There's a disk image: SillyBusAI-Installer.dmg. Drag Silly Bus AI.app out of it onto your Desktop, then double-click it.

macOS will refuse it the first time — “Apple could not verify Silly Bus AI is free of malware”. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway next to the line about Silly Bus AI. Once only.

The one-line install above exists precisely so you don't have to do any of that.
